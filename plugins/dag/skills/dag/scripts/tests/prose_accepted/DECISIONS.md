# Decisions

## D01 the shape of the predicate

The orchestrator, having considered the several competing formulations of the predicate and the various ways in which each of them might be defeated by an artifact that was written in good faith but under a different reading of the requirement, and having also weighed the cost of the alternative in which the check is deferred until a later wave when the evidence would be cheaper to gather but the repair would be very much more expensive to apply across the units that had by then already been dispatched, concluded that the narrower formulation should be preferred on balance for now.
